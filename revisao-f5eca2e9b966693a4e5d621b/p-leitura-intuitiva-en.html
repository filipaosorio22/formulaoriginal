Who are you when you stop being what the world expects of you?

There is a way of moving through life that is uniquely yours.

It doesn't show itself only in your rhythm or your energy; it shows in the patterns you repeat, the choices you make, the stories you tell yourself, the challenges you meet and the way you've grown used to responding to them.

It also shows in the relationships you create, in how you work and rest, in how you position and express yourself in the world; and in ways that are harder to explain: a dream that stays with you, a synchronicity that touched you, or a feeling you can't yet put into words.

Over time, you absorbed beliefs, references and ways of functioning that made you see life through certain lenses. About love, worth, safety, success, freedom…

Without realizing it, you began to move between your truth and what you believe the world expects of you, but you know you are more than those stories and more than the ways you found to adapt and even to belong.

The Essence Reading is an invitation to come back to your center. I weave together the symbolic language of your birth chart and your Human Design with what you share about your moment, to explore possible patterns, beliefs and dynamics and help you recognize what you want to keep cultivating and where you wish to choose differently in your life.

What you believe about yourself shapes your choices more than you imagine!

Astrology and a deeper look with Human Design

The Astrology I use has an alchemical reading. In the alchemical approach I follow, planets represent functions and movements, signs give them a quality of expression and houses place the themes in the different areas of life. I'm interested in the conversation between these elements and what it can help you explore in your experience. As the maxim attributed to Hermes Trismegistus says, “that which is below is like that which is above.”

Human Design combines astrological references with the I Ching, the Kabbalah and the chakra system in a language of its own for self-knowledge. It offers another language for exploring your rhythm, how you decide and how you relate. In this system, planetary positions correspond to activations of gates and lines, which I interpret as symbolic references to deepen that reflection.

“And then, what do I do with this information?”

The Reading is not a set of characteristics for you to keep about yourself. What makes it useful is the possibility of relating the images and questions you find to concrete situations in your life.

For example, the Sun in Capricorn can open up a reflection on your relationship with work, responsibility or success. Rather than being left with the idea that you're a responsible or persistent person, it's worth asking: What does fulfilment mean to you? When does commitment turn into overload? Are there moments when you feel you always have to do more to have worth?

Human Design can add other questions to this exploration. If your design shows emotional authority, for example, we can look at the relationship between what you feel and the moment you decide. What changes when you don't respond right away? What remains once the initial enthusiasm or discomfort passes? In what situations do you feel pressure to decide before it's your ideal moment?

It's not about concluding that a position in the chart explains your overload or that an authority determines the right way for you to decide. It's about using these languages to observe your experience more attentively: recognizing what happens, questioning habitual interpretations and discovering other possible responses.

You don't need to change everything at once, nor turn the Reading into yet another demand about how you should be. You can choose one question, carry it with you for a few days and notice what you discover. What do you recognize? What doesn't fit? What changes when you try responding differently?

If you'd like to go deeper into what you discover, or find it hard to do things differently even after recognizing a pattern, you can do that in the sessions with me. We start from concrete situations in your life to explore the beliefs, emotions and circumstances involved, drawing on the tools that make sense for what we agree to work on.

To go deeper

Alignment (and deepening) session

Sometimes understanding a pattern isn't enough for us to respond differently. We can recognize what's happening and still find it hard to say no, make a decision, express a need or move forward with something we want.

In this session, we start from a concrete situation in your life to explore the beliefs, emotions and circumstances involved and the possibilities you want to start building. You don't need to arrive with everything organized: we can start with a question, a conflict or something you feel but can't yet explain.

1Name it

Giving words to what's happening: what you feel, how you respond and what you believe about yourself or others in that situation. We can recognize expectations, learned rules or demands you had never stopped to question.

2Understand

Exploring how those responses may have made sense in your story, what they seek to protect and what it costs you to keep them today. Without treating your ways of protecting yourself as flaws, or assuming there's a single explanation for what you're going through.

3Experiment

Finding a perspective you want to cultivate and a possible step to take into your life: expressing a need, communicating a boundary or trying a different response in a familiar situation. Not as an imposed task, but as a possibility that makes sense to you.

The practices I bring in are chosen according to what you bring and what we agree on.

The session offers a space for listening and working together, so you can explore how to carry the new perspectives into your life, into the concrete situations of your moment.

If you've already received your Reading, we don't start from zero. I know your charts and the context you shared, and we can start from what it stirred up: something you recognized yourself in, an interpretation you wanted to question, or a difficulty that became clearer. It isn't just about explaining more elements of the charts, but about exploring what all of this means in your experience.

Questions you may still have
Do I need to know astrology or Human Design?

No. You don't need to know technical language or follow the chart's design. The Reading is presented in an accessible way, with links to practical life. All you need is to come with curiosity and openness to get to know yourself better.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Ask someone who might know, like a family member, or request your birth certificate, where the time is usually recorded. The time influences important elements of the chart, and in Human Design even more. If you still can't get it, talk to me before going ahead, so we can see what's possible to explore with the details you have.

What makes this Reading different?

I don't hand you a list of meanings of signs and planets. The Reading is intuitive, because my sensitivity takes part in how I recognize associations and build the interpretation. And it is archetypal, because I work with human images and themes, such as safety, freedom, desire, belonging and creation, without reducing you to a definition. I don't want you to be left just thinking “this describes me”, but asking “where do I recognize this and what can I try differently?”.

Does the Reading explain every element of my charts?

No. It isn't a complete report of all positions, aspects, centres, channels or gates. The theme you bring, the context you share and my intuitive interpretation guide which elements I go deeper into. Some may receive more attention and others may not be addressed, depending on their relevance to the Reading.

Why a story?

Because a story connects what a list separates. Instead of isolated characteristics, you follow someone who wants something, meets an obstacle and explores possibilities. The characters give shape to tensions you may recognize in yourself. The story is a complement to the Reading, created from the themes of your chart and what you share. It isn't your biography or a prediction: you can recognize yourself, disagree or give it another meaning.

How and when do I receive the Reading?

In writing, within 14 working days of payment confirmation. You can read in your own time, pause, repeat and return to the passages that touch you, as many times as you like.

Is there a live session?

The Reading is written. If you choose the Reading with alignment session, we meet after you've read it, in a session lasting 1 hour: we start from what you recognized, explore patterns and beliefs and shape next steps. You don't need to prepare anything, but you can bring notes and examples.

Will the Reading tell me what to do or predict the future?

No. It offers perspectives, questions and practical suggestions, not decisions or predictions. The chart is not a sentence, it's a starting point. You can recognize yourself, disagree or give another meaning to what you read: your experience is essential.

What is the Solar Return?

Every year, near your birthday, the Sun returns to exactly the position it was in when you were born. The chart of that moment is called the Solar Return and marks the start of a new yearly cycle in your life. In the Reading with Solar Return (+100€), I look at that chart in relation to your natal chart and to what you're living. If your birthday is approaching (about 1 month before), I analyze the new cycle; if you're in the middle of a cycle, I analyze what you're living now and the impacts of your year. You can request it at any time.

Is Human Design included?

Yes. Human Design is always part of the Reading, together with your birth chart: it's not an extra and it can't be bought separately. Astrology speaks of the archetypes and dynamics of your life; Human Design offers another language for exploring your rhythm and how you relate to decisions. In Human Design, I can explore elements such as type, strategy, authority, profile and other points of the design, depending on their relevance to the theme you bring.

Can I give a Reading to someone?

Yes. You only need the birth details of the person receiving it. You can give a card, which the person uses to book with me within 1 month, or the Reading already prepared, in writing. See how to give one.

If this is the Reading you were looking for

The way you see yourself influences the life you build, and none of those stories is definitive. Let's look at your chart and your moment together, with depth and lightness.
