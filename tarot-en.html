When what you desire doesn’t arrive

It’s not a lack of will. It’s what lies underneath

In my experience, when what you desire isn’t happening, it may be one of these reasons:

  • What you expect is not what you desire: what you expect, deep down, can gain more strength.
  • It doesn’t feel safe to receive yet: a part of you learned that having more can cost you dearly.
  • Your state holds back your request: you ask from a place of hurry, fear or exhaustion.
  • The step is too big: the body needs to feel safe in order to move forward.

In the Now Reading I look at your specific situation: what you share, the reading of your field and the symbolic analysis. You receive insights and perspectives on what is happening and concrete action proposals. The path and the choices always remain yours.

Before you choose

Questions you may have

Do I need to believe in anything?
No. You can approach this language with curiosity, without deciding yet what you believe in.
Read my perspective on the Readings.
Do I need to know exactly what to ask?
No. You can start with a theme or situation. The context you share helps me choose where to look and shape the questions we will explore.
Will the Reading tell me what to do or predict the future?
My approach does not treat symbols as certainties about a fixed future. I am interested in the present moment, the perspectives you can consider and what is within your reach. The choices remain yours.
Is it a live session?
No. You share the context in the form and I prepare and record the Reading. You receive an audio to listen to whenever it suits you, without needing to arrange a time: it is a kind of asynchronous session.
When do I receive the Reading?
Within 7 business days after your booking is confirmed and the necessary context is received.
What is the difference from the Essence Reading?
The Now Reading focuses on a specific theme or situation: you receive my interpretation and my perspective on what you share, in an in-depth audio. The Essence Reading allows a broader and deeper dive: I cross your charts to explore patterns, beliefs and different dynamics in your life, with more connections between different areas of your life and practical proposals. The choice depends on your moment and on the depth you want to explore now. See both options side by side.

The Reading is not a diagnosis and does not replace specialised support when that support is needed.
