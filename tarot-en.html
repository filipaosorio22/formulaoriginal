How I approach Tarot

The story of a coin tossed into the air.

My mother taught me that when we toss a coin to make a decision, the most revealing part is often how we feel when we see the result.

That is how I see Tarot: a symbolic language that can support reflection. The meaning of each card takes shape through the question, the context, its position and its relationship with the other cards.

My intuition participates in this interpretation, alongside my study of symbols and what you tell me. I do not read a card in isolation or apply the same meaning to everyone. My intuitive listening, as in my earlier Aura Readings, helps shape the perspective arising from the symbols, your context and what I sense. My aim is to help you ask new questions and listen to what begins to emerge within you.

If you’ve never looked at a card this way

The same symbol. More than one interpretation.

Tarot is a deck of 78 illustrated cards, organised into the Major and Minor Arcana. I use the images as a language for reflecting on human experiences: choosing, beginning, creating, setting boundaries or moving through change.

That is what I mean by archetypes: images that give shape to themes we may recognise in our lives. You don’t need to know the cards or hold a particular belief to explore what they make you think or feel.

Choice · values · relationship

The Lovers

The name may immediately bring love to mind. But this card can also open up reflection on choices, what you value and how you reconcile different desires.

In a question about a relationship, it may invite you to look at how you connect with someone and what you choose to nurture in that bond.

“What do I want to experience in this relationship, and what do I need to honour in myself?”

We can also explore a tension: am I choosing freely, out of fear of loss or to meet other people’s expectations?

These are examples of possible questions, not a reading for the person visiting this page. In an actual reading, I consider the spread, the other cards and the context you share. A card alone does not determine what will happen or how another person feels.

Before you choose

Questions you may have.

Do I need to believe in Tarot?
No. You can approach this language with curiosity, without deciding what you believe. Conhece a minha perspetiva sobre as leituras.
Isn’t it so general that anyone could recognise themselves?
A card on its own can suggest many meanings. I consider your question, context, the spread and the relationships between cards. You are invited to question my interpretation: you do not have to accept a phrase you do not recognise.
Do I need to know exactly what to ask?
No. You can begin with a theme or situation. The context you share helps me choose the spread and shape the questions we explore.
Will the reading tell me what to do or predict the future?
My approach does not treat cards as certainties about a fixed future. I’m interested in the present moment, the perspectives you can consider and what is within your reach. The choices remain yours.
Is it a live session?
No. You share your context in the form and I prepare and record the reading. You receive audio to listen to whenever suits you, without arranging a session time.
When will I receive my reading?
Within 5 business days after payment is confirmed and the context needed to prepare your reading is received.
Do you need my birth details?
For a Moment Reading, the starting point is the theme and context you share. The form does not ask for the birth details used in a Essence Reading.
How does this differ from a Essence Reading?
Tarot focuses on a specific question or situation, with my interpretation and perspective in audio. The Essence Reading is a broader exploration of your charts, patterns, beliefs and life dynamics, with a symbolic story and practical suggestions. Your choice depends on your moment and the depth you want to explore. Compare the two readings.

A reading is not a diagnosis and does not replace psychological, medical, legal or financial support when needed.
