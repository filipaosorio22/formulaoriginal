Before you listen

I won’t tell you what the future holds.

If astrology or Tarot makes you think of predictions or generic statements, I understand your hesitation. I do not believe a sign explains everything about you, or that a card or chart decides for you.

I use these languages as symbolic starting points for exploring your experience. You can listen, question and keep only what makes sense to you.

How I approach readings

Symbols open the conversation. Your experience gives it context.

A reading brings together a symbolic language, my interpretation and what you share about yourself.

Not everything familiar still fits. Sometimes the way we work, relate or use our time reflects what we learned to do to feel accepted and safe, rather than what we want today.

Intuition is part of this process. I combine the study of symbols with sensitivity, emerging associations and your context. I explain my interpretation so you can follow it and give it your own meaning.

Rather than a fixed answer, you may find a question worth returning to.

Reiki dedicated to your theme

Both readings include Reiki dedicated to the theme you share. For me, Reiki is an offering of unconditional loving energy. I dedicate a moment of intention, presence and care to your theme. It is an invitation to receive and integrate what the reading has brought up, at your own pace.

For me, knowing yourself does not mean finding a definitive version of who you are. It means understanding what you value and where you have room to choose. A reading comes to life when something you recognise reaches your everyday life: a conversation, a boundary or a small change.
