Cards · charts · another perspective

Intuitive Readings

There is more than one way to listen to yourself.

A Reading can help you see what you are experiencing from another angle. Cards and charts offer different starting points, with the same purpose: to create space for new perspectives and help you discover what makes sense in your life.

Before you listen

I won’t tell you what the future holds.

I have little interest in telling you “this is who you are” or “this is how you should act”. I am far more interested in exploring with you: “how do you experience this, what meaning do you give it and what alternatives exist?”

This is how I use Astrology, Human Design, Tarot, dream analysis and any other tool, because it is never about the tools.

Intuition is also part of the way I read: I connect my study of symbols with sensitivity, the images and associations that arise, and the context you bring. I explain how I arrive at an interpretation and distinguish what you share from what I offer as a possibility.

You do not need to believe in these languages to explore them, but it matters that you feel comfortable with a symbolic approach. You may recognise yourself, disagree or discover a question you had not asked before. A chart does not sum up who you are. A card does not decide what will happen. And my interpretation has no more authority than your experience.

My way of reading

I do not look only at each piece.
I am interested in the relationship between them.

I start with what brings you to the Reading, select the relevant elements, connect themes and look for a common thread, rather than accumulating descriptions of you.

One theme can touch several areas of your life. A question about work may open a conversation about recognition, security or freedom. I explore those connections without assuming everything has the same origin.

I shape the Reading through explanations, images and, when it makes sense, a symbolic story that brings seemingly separate elements together. Then I make that interpretation concrete: where might you observe this theme, which question deserves attention, and what possibility could you explore?

More than a fixed answer, you find questions worth returning to.

Both Readings include Reiki

Both Readings include distance Reiki, dedicated to the theme you share.

Reiki is a transmission of the energy of unconditional love. It can involve laying on hands or take place at a distance, as it does here, without touch or physical presence.

In my practice, I experience it as a gesture of unconditional love: a moment of intention, presence and care for you and the theme you bring. It is an energetic dimension that accompanies the Reading, without seeking to direct what you should feel or what must happen.

You do not need to feel anything specific, and there is no right experience to have.

Permission slips

The tools do not give you permission.
They help you give it to yourself.

I use the expression permission slips to describe something that helps us give ourselves permission to recognise or live a possibility we had not allowed ourselves to explore. This is how I see the tools I use and the Reading itself.

A chart, a symbol, a story or an interpretation can put words to what you feel, reveal a connection you had not seen or help you question something you took for granted. Perhaps recognising a theme in your chart helps you honour your own rhythm. Perhaps an image lets you consider a desire without immediately dismissing it. Perhaps a question creates room to try a different way of standing your ground.

The chart does not become an authority that grants you permission. But finding a language that resonates with you can make it easier to recognise that possibility as your own. It can bridge the gap between understanding that another path exists and feeling that you can try it too.

I do not want the Reading to become another condition: “I can only do this if my chart says yes.” I want it to be a space where you can recognise possibilities, question limits you have learned and choose what makes sense to you.

The Reading can offer a point of support.
The permission remains yours.
