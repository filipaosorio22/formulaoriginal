Before you listen

I won’t tell you what the future holds for you

Nor will I tell you “you are like this” or “you should act like that”. What I do through my Readings is explore with you: “how do you live this, what meaning do you give it and what alternatives exist?”

It is with this view that I use all the languages I have learned to read, as a translation of the symbolism of the unconscious. It is never about the tools, it is about what your unconscious is trying to show you, because that is where the answers you are looking for lie: in what keeps you out of alignment with what you desire, without you being aware of it.

Intuition is also part of the way I read: I combine the study of symbols with sensitivity to read the images and the associations with the context you bring; I share interpretations and distinguish what you share from what I propose as possibilities.

It is about allowing yourself other perspectives, raising questions and opening yourself to the answers.

Symbolic analysis brings images and questions, but I don’t read a symbol in isolation or apply the same meaning from one Reading to the next. My aim is to help you ask questions you may not have asked yet and to listen closely to what begins to emerge in you.

My way of reading

I don’t only look at each piece.
I’m interested in how they relate

I start from what brings you to the Reading, select the relevant elements, cross themes and look for a common thread, instead of piling up descriptions and information about you that lead nowhere.

One theme can touch several areas of your life. A question about work can open a conversation about recognition, security or freedom. I explore these links without assuming everything has the same origin, while elevating awareness of their possible connections.

More than a closed answer, you find questions worth returning to.

Every Reading includes Reiki

Reiki is a sending of unconditional loving energy. I dedicate a moment of intention, presence and care to you and to the theme you share, without trying to direct what you should feel or what has to happen. You don’t need to feel anything specific and there is no right experience to have.
