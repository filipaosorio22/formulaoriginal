Permission slips

The tools do not give you permission.
They help you give yourself permission.

I use the term permission slips for things that help us give ourselves permission to recognize or explore possibilities we may not have allowed ourselves to consider. This is how I see the tools I use in my work.

The chart isn’t an authority that grants you permission. Finding language that resonates with you can make it easier to recognize a possibility as your own and explore different paths and perspectives through it.

A reading can support you, but only you can give yourself permission.

Who you’ll be working with

I’m Filipa Osório

One side of me enjoys organizing ideas, asking questions, and finding a strategic direction. My background is in management, and that practical perspective informs my work.
In recent years, I’ve also made more room for my intuitive side. I’ve explored it through my own personal development and by supporting others, as well as through a deeper study and practice of symbolic languages, which continue to surprise me.

My work takes shape where these two worlds meet.
I enjoy finding the thread that connects things that may seem separate: what you think and feel, the choices you make, and the life you want to build. I bring this perspective to my work by making connections, broadening perspectives, and turning reflection into concrete possibilities.

In CEEN, we explore your business in relation to who you are and the life you want to build.
In readings and sessions, we explore questions about your personal life and open up new paths.
In business or in life, you are the starting point.
