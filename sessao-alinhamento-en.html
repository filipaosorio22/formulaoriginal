Who it is for

You can bring any theme you like

  • Romantic relationshipsYou repeat the same kind of relationship, or you don’t know what you really want from one.
  • Professional decisionsYou have a decision to make and doubts about the path you want to follow.
  • RoutineYour routine no longer serves you and you don’t know how to change it.
  • The timing of lifeYou compare your pace with other people’s and feel your life is out of time.
  • AbundanceIn some areas of life (money, love, time, health) things flow, and in others they always seem to cost more.
  • FriendshipsYou give more than you receive, or feel you have drifted from the people who matter to you.
  • ParentingYou react with your children the way your parents reacted with you, and you want to do it differently.
  • Patterns that repeatYou see the same scene come back, with different people and at different stages.
  • Fear of being judgedYou fear other people’s gaze and judgement when you want to express yourself.
  • CommunicationIt is hard for you to say clearly what you feel and what you need.

All themes have a common denominator, which is you: the way you think, what you believe, your story and your experience.
That is why, when we talk about one theme, doors open to many others that did not even seem connected.

The idea behind the session

Why I call it alignment

Much of what you live today comes from beliefs you learned early and patterns that repeat without you noticing: in how you respond, choose, expect or avoid.

When what you think, what you feel and what you do point in different directions, the path costs more. The Alignment Session was created to bring them back together: to understand where what repeats comes from and to find a step that makes sense for you, at your own pace.

You can book the session and start directly from the theme you want to work on.
