Alignment session (and deeper exploration)

Sometimes understanding a pattern is not enough to respond differently. We may recognise what happens and still find it difficult to say no, decide, express a need or move towards something we want.

We begin with a specific situation in your life. You do not need to have everything worked out: it can be a question, a conflict or something you feel but cannot yet explain.

Read people’s experiences ↓
What we explore

From what you are experiencing to a possible next step.

01

Nomear

Find words for what happens: what you feel, how you respond and what you believe in that situation.

02

Understand

Explore what those responses seek to protect and what it costs you to keep them today, without treating them as failings.

03

Try

Find a perspective to cultivate and a possible next step: express a need, communicate a boundary or respond differently.

The practices I bring depend on your theme and what we agree together. The starting point is your present moment.

How you can begin

With a reading or with your own theme.

You can book just the session and begin directly with what you want to explore. If you have already received a reading, we can begin with what it brought up.
