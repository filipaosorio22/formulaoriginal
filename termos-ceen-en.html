3. Formats, service description and process

CEEN is available in two formats, with different levels of depth, duration and pricing. The client chooses the format when booking.

3.1 CEEN Session

One-to-one work during a single meeting, focused on what the client brings and within the time available. It includes:

  • the client sharing essential context in advance and, where relevant, one key item, such as a sales page, a pricing table or the description of an offering;
  • a brief review of that context or material by Filipa Osório before the meeting;
  • a one-to-one online session lasting up to 2 hours, during which analysis, recommendations and next steps are explored.

Within 5 working days of the session, Filipa Osório provides the client with a short summary in Notion of what was covered, along with action points. The summary records the work done during the session and does not constitute new analysis.

The CEEN Session does not include further analysis after the meeting, implementation or asynchronous support. The work is carried out during the session itself, within the time available.

3.2 CEEN Cycle

Two one-to-one sessions, with personalized analysis carried out by Filipa Osório between them, focusing on the priority topics and materials that can be explored in depth within the working time included. It is not an exhaustive review of the entire business. It includes:

  • a first one-to-one online session lasting up to 2 hours;
  • an optional digital space for observation and sharing, made available for the three days following the first session, where the client may add examples, materials or relevant information;
  • a period of up to 21 working days during which Filipa Osório analyzes the priority topics and materials, connects her observations and prepares a personalized proposal;
  • a second one-to-one online session in which that proposal is presented, explained and discussed;
  • delivery of a personalized Notion space containing the personalized proposal.

The CEEN Cycle is considered complete once the second session has taken place. After that session, no further work, changes or adjustments by Filipa Osório to the client’s Notion space are included.

Use of the digital space for observation and sharing is optional. Choosing not to use it does not affect the client’s entitlement to the remaining stages of the service. In that case, the analysis is based on the information shared during the first session.

Provisions applicable to both formats

Other than the essential information requested when booking, no form needs to be completed. Once the booking has been confirmed, the client only needs to attend the session or sessions at the scheduled date and time and, where applicable, share the key material with the advance notice requested during the booking process.
