2. Nature of the service

CEEN is a one-to-one process focused on clarity, strategy and energy, designed for entrepreneurs, independent professionals and content creators who already have an established activity, through a business or personal brand, and sell services, sessions, programs, courses or products based on their knowledge and experience.

CEEN:

  • is not technical consultancy in management, marketing, finance, accounting or operations;
  • is not legal, financial or medical advice;
  • does not replace psychological or psychiatric support;
  • does not include technical audits, copywriting or implementation by Filipa Osório;
  • does not guarantee specific financial, commercial or personal outcomes.

The recommendations provided are based on the reality of the client’s business, the materials analyzed and an understanding of the client’s beliefs, patterns, desires, capacity and approach to decision-making, and are intended to support the client’s own decision-making. Final decisions and their implementation always remain the client’s responsibility.

3. Formats, service description and process

CEEN is available in two formats: the single session and the 3 sessions in a month. In both, strategic suggestions and the analysis of elements of the business that the client presents are worked on during the meetings. Neither format includes follow-up after the sessions. The client chooses the format when booking.

3.1 3 CEEN sessions in a month

One-to-one work across three meetings, over one month, centered on the business questions brought by the client. It includes:

  • the client sharing essential context in advance and, when relevant, a main piece of material (for example, a sales page, a price list or the description of an offer);
  • Filipa Osório reading the shared context before the first meeting;
  • three individual online sessions, over one month, in which strategic suggestions and, when relevant, the analysis of elements of the business presented by the client are worked on;
  • within up to 5 business days after the third session, a short summary and the action points in Notion, at no additional cost.

The 3 sessions must be held within one month from the date of the first session; those not used within that period are lost, with no refund. The service is considered completed with the third session and the delivery of the summary. The 3 CEEN sessions do not include follow-up after the sessions, later analysis of materials, implementation or any additional contact. The work focuses on the agreed questions.

3.2 1 session

One individual online session of 1h30, in which the priority chosen in the session is worked on, starting from what the client brings. It includes:

  • a brief review of the context shared by the client when booking;
  • one individual online session of 1h30, in which the analysis, recommendations and next steps are worked on;
  • within 5 business days after the session, a short summary and action points in Notion, at no additional cost.

The service is considered fully performed once the session has taken place. The 1 session does not include a plan between sessions, analysis of materials, implementation or support afterwards.
