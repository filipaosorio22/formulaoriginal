3. Formats, service description and process

CEEN is available in two formats, which differ in how the work is carried out: in the Session, the analysis and recommendations are developed during the meeting; in the Cycle, the work also includes analysis of the agreed materials and preparation of a proposal between two meetings. The client chooses the format when booking.

3.1 CEEN Session

One-to-one work during a single meeting, centered on one priority question brought by the client. It includes:

  • the client sharing essential context in advance and, where relevant, one key item, such as a sales page, a pricing table or the description of an offering;
  • a brief review of that context or material by Filipa Osório before the meeting;
  • a one-to-one online session lasting up to 2 hours, during which analysis, recommendations and next steps are explored.

Within five working days of the session, Filipa Osório provides the client with a short summary in Notion of what was covered, together with any action points identified during the session, at no additional cost. The summary records the work done during the session and does not constitute any new analysis. The service is considered fully performed once the session has taken place and the summary has been delivered.

The CEEN Session does not include further analysis after the meeting, implementation or asynchronous support. The work is centered on the question agreed for the meeting.

3.2 CEEN Cycle

Two one-to-one sessions, with analysis carried out by Filipa Osório between them on one central question brought by the client. The aim of the analysis, the materials included and what falls outside it are agreed in the first session. It is not a review of the entire business. Any new request that requires additional work falls outside the Cycle and is agreed separately. It includes:

  • a first one-to-one online session lasting up to 2 hours;
  • a three-day period following the first session during which the client must submit the agreed materials needed for the analysis and may, if they wish, share additional information related to the focus of the Cycle through a dedicated digital workspace;
  • a period of up to 21 working days, starting at the end of that three-day period, during which Filipa Osório analyzes the agreed materials, connects her observations and prepares a personalized proposal;
  • a second one-to-one online session in which that proposal is presented, explained and discussed;
  • delivery of a personalized Notion space containing the personalized proposal.

The CEEN Cycle is considered complete once the second session has taken place. After that session, no further work, changes or adjustments by Filipa Osório to the client’s Notion space are included.

The client must submit the agreed materials needed for the analysis. If any materials are not submitted within the three-day period, the analysis will proceed on the basis of the information shared during the first session and the materials received. The 21-working-day timeframe will remain unchanged.

Sharing additional information is optional. Choosing not to do so does not affect the client’s entitlement to the remaining stages of the service.

Provisions applicable to both formats

Other than the essential information requested when booking, no form needs to be completed. Once the booking has been confirmed, the client only needs to attend the session or sessions at the scheduled date and time and, where applicable, share the key material with the advance notice requested during the booking process.
