CEEN · Clarity, Strategy and Energy in Business

Your business is not separate from you

An approach to exploring your business and the person behind it, and identifying decisions that make sense for your current reality.

An approach that begins with you

Your business
can evolve with you

At CEEN, we don’t assume there’s anything wrong with you, that you need to become an ideal version of yourself, or that you have to follow a formula for success.

The starting point is you and your current reality: where you are, the business you’ve built, and what you want to change or build on.

See the whole picture without losing sight of what matters

Business decisions don’t happen in isolation from the person running the business. Strategy, how you work, what you value and the ideas guiding your choices influence one another.

In CEEN, we explore these connections to better understand what’s happening and identify concrete possibilities that align with who you are. Not to decide for you, but to help you decide with greater clarity.

Why I’m the one supporting you

I’ve been on the side of someone who changes their life and builds a business

I trained in Management and worked in that field for five years. In 2019, before the pandemic, I had a burnout, and that is when I became interested in how we create our lives, how the brain works, and in going deeper into my own personal and professional development.

In 2022 I resigned, left corporate management and trained in the areas of life that had always interested me: education and child development, and started my business in that field, working with children, mothers, fathers and teachers.

At the same time, I went deeper into personal development training and tools that allowed me to begin working with people in the different areas of their lives, while I was transforming myself in the process.

I also realised that everything I learned about childhood relates to the beliefs and patterns we live as adults, so I combined that knowledge with symbolic languages and created services aligned with me, which served people better because they were truly integrative.

On the path of building the business, I took dozens of marketing courses and I know the “shoulds” well: they helped me a lot, mainly to understand what I didn’t want for myself, and they reinforced the importance of being aligned with our business and having it be an extension of our energy.

That is where CEEN comes from, combining my training and experience in strategy. It is about finding your own way, without being swept along by other people’s marketing formulas, with someone beside you who respects your essence and helps you follow it at every step.

The CEEN promise

A business designed around you, rather than other people’s formulas

I’ve seen hundreds of people, especially women, try to force strategies into their businesses by following formulas and mentors without considering who they are, the life they want, or their personal energy. Without that foundation, no strategy is sustainable.

In CEEN, we work alongside you to open up new perspectives and understand what you really want. Only then do we build a strategy that’s unique to you, so you can take action in a way that feels aligned.
