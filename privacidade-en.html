1. Who processes your data

The controller of the personal data collected through this website and the services described on it is Filipa Osório, based in Porto, Portugal. For any question about this policy or your data, you can contact me at mail@filipaosorio.com.

2. Which services this policy applies to

This policy applies to the data collected through filipaosorio.com and in connection with the services presented here, namely CEEN · Clarity, Strategy and Energy in Business and the Leitura do Momento (Reading of the Moment, for yourself or as a gift, including the audio meditation), as well as conversations with schools and gatherings with communities and companies. The Terms and Conditions of the Reading of the Moment also apply to the Reading. The CEEN Terms and Conditions also apply to CEEN.

3. What data I collect

Depending on how you interact with me, I may collect:

  • Contact and booking data: name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number and the information you share when you request a service or book a conversation. For CEEN, you fill in a request (name, email, mobile number, information about your business, the question you want to work on, links and how you found me) and you choose the date on the Cal.com calendar. The free conversation for schools is booked on Cal.com.
  • Data shared in sessions: everything you tell me before, during and after the sessions, including in the digital space for observation and sharing (where applicable).
  • Payment data: I do not keep your card or bank account details. The Reading of the Moment is paid through Stripe, which tells me your name, email address and the payment made. CEEN is paid by MB WAY, bank transfer or Revolut. If you give your Portuguese tax number (NIF) in the request, I use it for the invoice.
  • Browsing data: on the CEEN page, through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (a tool that shows me, without identifying you, how the page is browsed), only if you accept that category of cookies (see section 7).
  • Reading of the Moment for yourself: before payment, you fill in a request with your name, email, WhatsApp number, date, time (as accurate as possible) and place of birth, what you are going through and, if you wish, what you already know about your birth chart and Human Design. Afterwards, I may contact you on WhatsApp and, if you wish, you can send me an audio message. Please avoid sharing other people’s data or sensitive information that is not needed.
  • Reading of the Moment as a gift: the buyer gives their name, email and WhatsApp number, the name of the person who will receive it and, if they wish, that person’s date of birth and a note about the gift. The Reading is only prepared after I have spoken with that person, whom I ask for the necessary data and context. If a Reading was given to you and you do not want it, you can ask me to delete your data.
  • Request without payment: the request reaches me when you send it, even if the payment is not completed, and I may contact you about it.
  • Speaking instead of typing: if you use that button in a form, your voice is converted into text by your own browser (in Chrome, for example, through Google’s services); I only receive the text, never the recording.
  • Record of the Reading conditions: your acceptance of the Terms and your request for the preparation to begin right after payment, in a box that is not pre-ticked, are recorded with the request. They serve to document the contract and to comply with legal obligations, with no automatic subscription to commercial communications. This policy does not replace the cancellation and refund conditions set out in the terms.
  • For gatherings with communities and companies: the name, email, phone number and organisation of the person arranging the gathering, and the information needed to prepare it. I do not collect data from participants, except when someone chooses to contact me directly or to share feedback.

5. Who I share your data with

I do not sell or share your data for third-party marketing purposes. For the website and services to work, I use the following providers, which process data on my behalf or as part of their own services:

  • Cal.com, booking and calendar management.
  • Stripe, payment processing.
  • Substack, newsletter. You subscribe yourself, directly on Substack, which processes your email under its own policy. If you indicate on the form that you want it, I only learn that you expressed that interest.
  • Web3Forms, delivery to my email of the answers to the website forms (Reading request, CEEN booking and invitations).
  • Notion, organising and sharing the documents and summaries of CEEN sessions and the CEEN Cycle.
  • Google Analytics, website visit statistics, only with consent.
  • Microsoft Clarity, understanding, without identifying you, how the page is browsed (where you stop, how far you scroll), only with consent.
  • Brevo, managing the contacts of people who previously signed up to an email list (section 10).
  • GitHub, website hosting, with technical access logs (such as the IP address).
  • Google Fonts, website typefaces: your browser requests them from Google’s servers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), direct communication with you, including sending proof of payment when booking CEEN, and in other situations where you choose this channel. I keep the history of these conversations as part of the record of the service provided, for the periods set out in section 8.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy, which governs how it processes data in its systems.

6. International transfers

Some of the providers above (namely Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Stripe, Cal.com, Notion, Web3Forms and Meta/WhatsApp) are based or process data outside the European Economic Area, particularly in the United States. In these cases, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, as implemented by each provider.

7. Cookies

Statistics and the booking calendar are only used on the CEEN page. When you first visit it, you are shown a notice where you can choose to accept, reject or manage your preferences in detail, by category:

  • Necessary, always active, allow the website’s basic operation and do not store identifiers.
  • Statistics (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity), only activated if you allow it. They help me understand how many people visit the website and how they browse it, without showing me who you are.
  • Booking calendar (Cal.com), only loads, with the cookies this involves, if you allow it.

So that it works as you expect, the website also stores in your browser a few preferences that do not identify you: light or dark theme, text size, whether you have already seen the gift card notice and whether the Reading request is for yourself or a gift.

Your cookie choice is stored in your own browser for 180 days, after which the notice appears again. You can change your choice at any time here: the cookie notice appears again so you can choose anew.

8. How long I keep your data

  • Invoicing documents: 10 years, as required by law (Portuguese Commercial Code and VAT Code).
  • Data relating to the services provided: up to 3 years after the service or support ends, unless you ask for deletion earlier or purchase a service again, in which case the period is reassessed from that moment.
  • Unpaid Reading requests: there is no automatic deletion period; they are kept for as long as needed to follow up on the request, and I delete them if you ask.
  • Browsing data (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity): according to each tool’s settings.
  • Email list: for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, I keep only your email address on a suppression list, to make sure I do not email you again.

9. Testimonials and feedback

I may share comments or messages you share with me as testimonials anonymously, without your name or voice and without details that could identify you, on the website or in other promotional materials. To use your name, voice (audio) or image, I always ask for specific permission, separate from the purchase of any service, stating the content and the channels where it will be published.

You may decline without any consequence, object to anonymous use and withdraw permission at any time by writing to mail@filipaosorio.com. In that case, the content is no longer used in new materials and is removed from the website.

10. Emails and newsletter

The newsletter is on Substack: you subscribe yourself, directly there. Subscribing is always optional.

If you previously signed up to one of my email lists (for example, when requesting a free chart), I keep your name, email and, if you gave it, your mobile number on the Brevo platform, based on your consent and for as long as the subscription is active.

You can unsubscribe through the link at the end of each email, or ask for access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to mail@filipaosorio.com.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data I hold about you;
  • request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request the deletion of your data, where applicable;
  • request the restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • request the portability of the data you have provided to me;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing;
  • lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at mail@filipaosorio.com.

12. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration. However, no system is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the bottom.
