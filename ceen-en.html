CEEN · Clarity, Strategy and Energy in Business

A new way of seeing yourself and your business
and of choosing how you want to be seen

Bring your business closer to who you are and
to your moment, allowing it to evolve with you

CEEN is a one-to-one process of analysis and guidance to help you review how you work and the place you take in your business, without separating these decisions from who you are and what you want to build, with your unique personal energy.

We start with one central question about the business, which can relate to others. As I listen, I pay attention to how you talk about your business, what excites you or holds you back and the weight you give to certain decisions. I ask questions and reflect back the connections I recognize in what I see and in what you share with me during the session.

This way of listening helps us recognize beliefs, fears and patterns that may be influencing your choices, even when you are not yet aware of them. We connect what we notice with the reality of your business and with what you want for yourself right now.

Sometimes, the biggest challenge is not building your business.
It is changing how you see yourself and your place within it.

Your business is at the heart of CEEN, but it is never considered in isolation from you.
Your identity, energy, capacity, desires and current stage of life help us recognize what still reflects who you are and how you want to continue working and evolving.

For people who are an essential part
of the value their business offers

CEEN is for independent professionals and for anyone running a business in which their knowledge, perspective and way of working are an essential part of the value it offers.

Your business is already up and running, but you want to review not only what you offer and how you present it, but also how you organize your weekly tasks, how you deliver your work and how you take your place in your business.

Or you feel the desire to make room for a new direction that is still hard to imagine as possible.

Your definition of professional success is yours alone

For you, professional success may mean growing, increasing your income or reaching more people. It may mean having more free time, more autonomy, recognition, impact or fulfilment.

Perhaps it is also making decisions with confidence and communicating authentically, or feeling more at ease with your work and no longer experiencing certain professional challenges as such an emotional drain.

You may already have achieved results that, for you, once represented success, yet still feel that the way you work today no longer reflects who you are. That does not diminish the value of what you have built. It may simply mean that it is time to reshape it so it can evolve with you.

🫧

In CEEN, we do not assume that there is something wrong with you, that there is an ideal version you should live up to, or a formula for success you have to follow. In CEEN, the best strategy is the one that makes sense for you! Alignment does not have to mean the absence of tension; a choice can make sense and still bring fear or discomfort. Just as reviewing what you have built does not diminish your journey; it simply allows you to choose how you want to continue.

Not every difficulty can be solved with a new strategy.

Sometimes, difficulty with charging, delegating or communicating is deeply connected to what that decision means to you, not simply to the lack of a plan.

In CEEN, we connect business decisions with who you are and what you are experiencing, because every choice is also influenced by what you want, what you fear and what you are willing and able to sustain, even if that is what success brings.

Clarity does not make the change for you, but it can reduce the time you spend pursuing a direction that drains you, putting off decisions, or trying to solve solely within your business something that also needs to be recognized within yourself.

CEEN helps you explore not only the direction of your business but also the way you want to work in it,
and what you need to recognize, decide and try in order to make room for that possibility.

🧩 Your business as a system

Your business is not just what you sell. It is also the place you occupy within it, the identity it communicates, the structure that supports it, the relationships it creates, and the balance between what you give and what you receive.

Looking at your business as a system means recognizing that your role, its history, identity, structure, relationships and exchanges all influence one another. We start from the question you bring and explore the connections that matter for that work.

Depending on your current situation, we may explore:

  • You and your role: how you see yourself, make decisions and take your place in the business;
  • Identity and positioning: how you want to be recognized;
  • Offerings and communication: what you sell, to whom, and how you present its value;
  • Structure and boundaries: how you organize the work, processes, delivery and responsibilities;
  • Belonging and relationships: the history and people that influence the business;
  • Balance in exchanges: what you give and receive in money, time and energy;
  • Sustainable growth: what can evolve without losing its identity or quality, or becoming increasingly dependent on you.

This perspective helps us recognize the connections that matter when deciding what to keep, reshape or transform, since these dimensions influence one another:

changing your offerings may call for different communication; growth may require a different structure; and a change within you may transform the place you want to occupy in your business.

More effort does not necessarily mean more income. Increasing your results at the expense of your time, willingness or vitality does not make growth sustainable, nor, quite likely, aligned with what you want.

⚡ Personal energy as strategic information

In CEEN, personal energy is not just about feeling energized or tired. It means the capacity available to you through the interaction of physical, mental, emotional, social and subtle dimensions, which influences your ability to create, decide, connect with others and take on responsibilities.

A professional decision does not depend on rational analysis alone. It is also influenced by what you feel you might gain, lose, prove or protect through it, often at an unconscious level. For example:

  • 💰 Money: security, freedom and choice, but also difficulty receiving, charging more or dealing with how others see you;
  • 👁️ Visibility: recognition and impact, but also exposure, criticism or judgment;
  • 🚀 Growth: new possibilities, but also greater responsibility or the fear of losing freedom and quality.

In CEEN, these elements are always connected to the concrete reality of your business and the choices ahead of you. In this way, we bring strategy, marketing, identity and intention closer together, for a business aligned with you.

What not to expect from CEEN

No. CEEN does not replace technical support in management, marketing, finance, accounting or operations. It also does not include technical audits, copywriting or implementation by me. I bring together what I observe in you and your business with my knowledge of strategy and marketing, as well as personal development, to offer tailored possibilities, criteria and recommendations.

I will help you recognize what may not yet be clear, offer recommendations and suggest possible directions. I do not decide or act for you: the final decisions and practical actions remain yours.

Are the sessions online or in person?

All CEEN sessions are one-to-one and online: both the Session and the two sessions included in the Cycle.

Does my business need to be structured?

No. Your business may be just starting, growing or being reorganized. If you are just starting, the CEEN Session is the format that makes sense, so we can clarify a central question and your first steps. The CEEN Cycle requires a business that is already up and running and engaging with the real world, with offerings, prices, communication, clients, sales or attempts to make sales, and concrete decisions we can explore.

Do I need to identify with a particular energy-based approach?

No. The energy dimension relates to what you are actually experiencing, which is not always immediately visible: attention, emotions, beliefs, habits, capacity and bodily responses. We always use only what makes sense for you.

How do I choose between the CEEN Session and the CEEN Cycle?

In the Session, we work on one central question during our meeting, through questions, analysis and recommendations developed together during the session. In the Cycle, we work on one central question across two meetings, with analysis of the agreed materials and a well-reasoned proposal prepared between them.

Both include a written record in Notion. With the Session, you receive a short summary of the work done during the meeting within five business days. With the Cycle, you receive a well-reasoned proposal based on the analysis carried out between the two meetings. The choice depends on the kind of work you are looking for, not the number of questions you bring.

You do not need a Session before the Cycle: you can book the CEEN Cycle directly if you are looking for analysis between meetings and a personalized proposal.

What questions can I bring?

You can bring a question about your business or a question of your own that you feel is affecting your business, as long as it relates to how you experience your business, make decisions or take your place within it. We can explore identity, beliefs, patterns, fears, visibility, money or responsibility when they are influencing your professional choices.

If you bring several questions, we agree on the priority for the session. We may explore relevant connections with the other questions, but the work remains focused on what we have agreed to address.

What happens after the meetings? Do I receive support?

In the CEEN Session, no new analysis is included after the meeting. The context review is brief and takes place before the meeting when needed. Analysis, recommendations and decisions are worked through during the session and, within 5 business days, you receive a short summary in Notion with the action points.

In the CEEN Cycle, the analysis focuses on the central question and the materials agreed in the first meeting, not on the whole business. Within three days of the first meeting, you send the agreed materials and, if you wish, additional information. During the analysis, I may ask focused questions or request clarification. These exchanges are intended to complete my understanding, not to support implementation.

Neither format includes implementation or ongoing support. Afterwards, you can move forward independently or book a new CEEN Session so we can keep working on other parts of your business.
