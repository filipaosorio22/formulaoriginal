CEEN is a one-to-one process of analysis and guidance to help you review your offerings, your communication or the way you work in your business, without separating these decisions from who you are and the life you want to build, grounded in your own unique personal energy.

You can come with a specific question or several areas you would like us to review together. As I listen, I pay attention to how you talk about your business, what excites you or holds you back, the weight you may attach to certain decisions, and any unconscious contradictions that may emerge.

This way of listening helps us recognise beliefs, fears and patterns that may be influencing your choices, even when you are not yet aware of them. We connect what we notice with the reality of your business and what you want to change.

I help you recognise what makes sense to keep, reshape or let go of, and offer concrete recommendations so you can decide on your next steps with greater confidence.

Sometimes, the biggest challenge is not building your business.
It is changing how you see yourself and your place within it.

Your business is at the heart of CEEN, but it is never observed separately from you.
Your identity, energy, capacity, desires and current stage of life help us recognise what still reflects who you are and how you want to continue working and evolving.

Your definition of professional success is yours

For you, professional success may mean growing, increasing your income or reaching more people. It may mean having more free time, more autonomy, recognition, impact or fulfilment. Or it may mean making decisions with confidence and communicating authentically, feeling more at ease with your work, and no longer experiencing certain professional challenges as such an emotional drain.

You may already have achieved results that represented success for you, yet still feel that the way you work today no longer reflects who you are. That does not diminish the value of what you have built. It may simply mean that it is time to reshape it so it can evolve with you.

🫧

In CEEN, we do not assume that there is something wrong with you or your business, that there is an ideal version to live up to, or a formula for success to follow. The best strategy is the one that fits you! Reviewing what you have built does not diminish your journey; it lets you choose how to continue.

Not every difficulty can be solved with a new strategy.

Sometimes, difficulty with charging, delegating or communicating is deeply connected to what that decision means to you, not simply to the lack of a plan.

In CEEN, we connect business decisions with who you are and what you are experiencing, because every choice is also influenced by what you want, what you fear and what you are willing and able to sustain.

Clarity does not make the change for you, but it can reduce the time you spend pursuing a direction that drains you, putting off decisions, or trying to solve solely within your business something that also needs to be recognised within yourself.

An outside perspective that sees beyond

When you are immersed in your business, it can be difficult to distinguish what still makes sense from what you maintain out of habit, fear or expectation. By reflecting back what I notice in the way you speak and asking questions about what seems contradictory, I help you see distinctions that may go unnoticed when you think things through on your own. Not to choose for you, but to help you recognise what you truly want to keep developing.

An integrated view of your business

If you would like to explore the approach in more depth, open the topics below: how different areas of your business influence one another, and how your energy shapes your professional decisions.

🧩 Your business as a system

Your business is not just what you sell. It is also the place you occupy within it, the identity it communicates, the structure that supports it, the relationships it creates, and the balance between what you give and what you receive.

Looking at your business as a system means considering, together, the person behind it, the identity that guides it, the structure that supports it, the relationships it creates, and the exchanges that allow it to continue.

Depending on your current situation, we may explore:

  • You and your role: how you see yourself, make decisions and take your place in the business;
  • Identity and positioning: how you want to be recognised;
  • Offerings and communication: what you sell, to whom, and how you present its value;
  • Structure and boundaries: how you organise the work, delivery and responsibilities;
  • Belonging and relationships: the history and people that influence the business;
  • Balance in exchanges: what you give and receive in money, time and energy;
  • Sustainable growth: what can evolve without losing its identity or quality, or becoming increasingly dependent on you.

These dimensions influence one another: changing your offerings may call for different communication; growth may require a different structure; and a change within you may transform the place you want to occupy in your business.

More effort does not necessarily mean more income. Increasing your results at the expense of your time, willingness or vitality does not make growth sustainable.

This perspective helps us recognise the connections that matter when deciding what to keep, reshape or transform, without analysing everything or looking for a single explanation.

⚡ Personal energy as strategic information

In CEEN, personal energy is not just about feeling energised or tired. It is the capacity available to you through the interaction of physical, mental, emotional, social and subtle dimensions, which influences your ability to create, decide, connect with others and take on responsibilities.

A professional decision does not depend on rational analysis alone. It is also influenced by what you feel you might gain, lose, prove or protect through it. For example:

  • 💰 Money: security, freedom and choice, but also difficulty receiving, charging more or dealing with how others see you;
  • 👁️ Visibility: recognition and impact, but also exposure, criticism or judgement;
  • 🚀 Growth: new possibilities, but also greater responsibility or the fear of losing freedom and quality.

In CEEN, these elements are always connected to the concrete reality of your business and the choices ahead of you. In this way, we bring strategy, marketing, identity and intention closer together.

All CEEN sessions are one-to-one and online: both the Session and the two sessions included in the Cycle.

Does my business need to be fully structured?

No. Your business may be at an early stage, growing or being reorganised, but it needs to be up and running and engaging with the real world. It is important to have offerings, prices, communication, clients, sales or attempts to make sales, and concrete decisions we can explore.

Do I need to identify with a particular energy-based approach?

No. The energy dimension relates to what you are actually experiencing, which is not always immediately visible: attention, emotions, beliefs, habits, capacity and bodily responses. We use only what makes sense for you.

Will you tell me exactly what to do?

I will help you recognise what may not yet be clear, offer recommendations and suggest possible directions. I do not decide or act for you: the final decisions and practical actions remain yours.

Can a CEEN Session focus on a more personal question?

Yes, as long as it relates to how you experience your business, make decisions or take your place within it. We can explore identity, beliefs, patterns, fears, visibility, money or responsibility when they are influencing your professional choices.

How do I know whether I need a CEEN Session or a CEEN Cycle?

The Session means working with me during our meeting: questions, analysis and recommendations in real time, with a short summary and action points in Notion within 5 working days. The Cycle makes sense if you also want me to dedicate time to analysis between meetings and receive a personalised proposal in Notion, with recommendations, priorities and the reasoning behind them. Both include a Notion deliverable, but only the Cycle includes analysis between meetings and a proposal with reasoning. The choice depends on the kind of work you are looking for, not the number of questions you bring.

Do I need to have a CEEN Session before booking the CEEN Cycle?

No. You can book the CEEN Cycle directly if you are looking for analysis between meetings and a personalised proposal.

Does the CEEN Session include analysis after the meeting?

No new analysis is included. The context review is brief and takes place before the meeting when needed. Analysis, recommendations and decisions are worked through during the session and, within 5 working days, you receive a short summary in Notion with the action points. Implementation and follow-up support are not included.

Does the CEEN Cycle analyse the whole business?

No. The analysis focuses on the priority topics and materials that can be explored in depth within the work included. I may connect different areas of your business, but the deliverable reflects what has actually been analysed, not an exhaustive review of everything.

What if I bring more than we can cover in a CEEN Session?

We work through what we can within the time available. If it makes sense to continue, we can consider another Session or the Cycle, where additional analysis of materials and a proposal would be useful. The decision is yours.

Do I receive support between sessions or after the Cycle?

The CEEN Cycle does not include ongoing support. Between sessions, you have three days to add relevant information. During the analysis, I may ask focused questions or request clarification to prepare the proposal. These exchanges are intended to complete my understanding, not to support implementation or respond continuously to day-to-day questions.

The Cycle is complete after the second session. You can then move forward independently or book a new CEEN Session to work through another specific question.

If I recognise that the process would benefit from closer support, I may suggest a CEEN Journey. This is a separate format, is not included in the Cycle, and is not available to book directly.
