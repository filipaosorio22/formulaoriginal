My story

I have always been close to children

Since I was littleFrom 5th grade on, when I moved to another playground, I would go to the little ones’ playground: I helped as much as I could, played with them, looked after them sometimes and ran the errands they asked me to. And I did babysitting from a very young age.
2019I had a burnout. I resigned to spend a year without working and started studying childhood and education. I volunteered at a nursery: I served lunches, was in the playgrounds, did whatever was needed.
2021I did babysitting every weekend, for different families.
2022I did the trainings I now have in early childhood and worked as a consultant in a nursery. I started working effectively with people, children included: in one-to-one and group sessions, and accompanying parents and teachers.

It is the area of my heart. But I realised that the work I do with adults is based much more on how each person is with themselves, the beliefs they carry and the way they see life.

For schools, nurseries and institutions

Talks and trainings

We are in the age of artificial intelligence. The world and the way we work are very different from a few years ago, and we do not know what the future will be. Preparing children for uncertainty is not worrying them: it is accepting that anything is possible. If memorising no longer makes sense, what remains is knowing who I am, what moves me, how I deal with what I feel and how I relate to others.

Change starts with the adult. When we know and explore our individuality, we become much more open to letting children live theirs, with their gifts and desires. For that, we need to reprogram the beliefs we were shaped by as children.
