2. Nature of the service

The Reading of the Moment is an interpretive and reflective practice, created from the client and what they are going through. It is based on an intuitive and symbolic reading of the client’s energy field (aura) and a foundation about their energy, drawn from birth data and the language of Human Design. Other tools, chosen by Filipa Osório according to the client’s moment, may also be included, such as astrology, Tarot, a systemic view inspired by family constellations, belief work inspired by ThetaHealing® and mental reprogramming. Not every tool is used in every reading, and this is not a full reading of each chart, a family constellation or a ThetaHealing® session.

The Reading of the Moment:

  • is not a prediction of the future and does not determine events;
  • is not a diagnosis or medical, psychological, legal or financial advice;
  • does not replace psychological, psychiatric or medical care;
  • does not guarantee specific personal, professional or financial outcomes.

What is presented in the Reading is intended to support the client’s own reflection. Decisions, and how they are put into practice, are always the client’s responsibility.

3. Service description and process

The Reading of the Moment includes:

  • a personalised audio reading, made from what the client shares, to listen to at their own pace;
  • the aura reading and a foundation about the client’s energy (Human Design), and the other tools Filipa Osório considers relevant to the client’s moment;
  • a Reiki sending dedicated to the client and the themes they share. Reiki is presented as a sending of unconditional love energy, a moment of intention, presence and care. It is a complementary spiritual practice, within the interpretive and reflective nature described in section 2.

Before payment, the client fills in the request on the website (for themselves or as a gift), stating what they are going through, in writing or using the speech-to-text function, and their date, time and place of birth. If the client does not know their time of birth, they should speak with Filipa Osório before buying. After receiving the request, Filipa Osório may contact the client on WhatsApp, and the client may, if they wish, add an audio message.

The Reading is delivered as audio within 10 business days from confirmation of payment and receipt of all the necessary details and context. It works as an asynchronous session and does not include a live session. The service is considered completed with the delivery of the audio.

Provider’s decision not to carry out the Reading. Filipa Osório reserves the right not to start, or to interrupt, the preparation of the Reading, before its delivery, if she considers that the conditions to provide the service with the necessary care and suitability are not met. In that case, she informs the client by email and refunds the amount paid in full, at no cost to the client.
