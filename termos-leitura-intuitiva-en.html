Intuitive Reading is a spiritual and interpretive practice of self-knowledge. It uses the birth chart and Human Design as symbolic languages to explore the moment and the theme the client shares, offering perspectives, questions and practical suggestions.

Intuitive Reading:

  • is not a prediction of the future and does not determine events;
  • is not a diagnosis or medical, psychological, legal or financial advice;
  • does not replace psychological, psychiatric or medical support;
  • does not guarantee specific personal, professional or financial outcomes.

What is presented in the Reading and in the session is intended to support the client’s own reflection. Decisions, and how they are put into practice, always remain the client’s responsibility.

3. Formats, service description and process

Through the booking form, the client provides the information needed for the Reading: date, time and place of birth, and what they are going through or the theme they want to bring. If the client does not know their time of birth, they should speak with Filipa Osório before purchasing.

3.1 Intuitive Reading

  • a personalized Reading of the client’s birth chart and Human Design, guided by the theme and context they share;
  • a short personalized symbolic story connected to the client’s moment, which gives shape to the themes explored;
  • an extra personalized audio, solely to integrate the Reading.

The Reading is delivered within 14 business days of confirmation of payment and receipt of all the necessary information. The Reading and the story are delivered in writing, the audio is an integration complement, and the base Reading does not include a live meeting. The service is considered fully performed once the written Reading has been delivered.

3.2 Intuitive Reading + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.1 and, after the Reading has been delivered, a one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório to go deeper into the themes the client recognized, work on the relevant beliefs and, if the client wishes, explore their Human Design chart in more depth. The session is scheduled after the Reading has been delivered. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.3 Intuitive Reading + Solar Return

Includes everything described in 3.1 and an additional written Reading dedicated to the client’s yearly cycle (Solar Return), related to their birth chart and the moment they are living. If the client’s birthday is near (about 1 month before or less), the new cycle is analysed; otherwise, the current cycle is analysed. Both Readings are delivered within the timeframe set out in 3.1. This format is considered complete once both Readings have been delivered.

3.4 Intuitive Reading + Solar Return + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.1 and 3.3 and, after the Readings have been delivered, a one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório, as set out in 3.2. The session is not scheduled at the time of purchase: date options are sent after the Reading has been delivered. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.5 Stand-alone alignment (and deepening) session

A one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório, of up to 1h30, without a prior Reading, which starts directly from the theme the client wants to work on. The session is not scheduled at the time of purchase: once payment has been confirmed, the client is taken to the booking page (Cal.com) to choose the date and time, and also receives the link by email. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

In that case, I will inform the client by e-mail and fully refund the amount paid for the services that will not be provided, at no cost to the client.
