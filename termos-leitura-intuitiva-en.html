Includes everything described in 3.1 and, after the reading has been delivered, a one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório to go deeper into the themes the client recognized, work on the relevant beliefs and, if the client wishes, explore their Human Design chart in more depth. The session is scheduled after the reading has been delivered. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.3 Leitura Intuitiva + Solar Return

Includes everything described in 3.1 and an additional audio reading dedicated to the client’s yearly cycle (Solar Return), related to their birth chart and the moment they are living. Both readings are delivered within the timeframe set out in 3.1. This format is considered complete once both readings have been delivered.
