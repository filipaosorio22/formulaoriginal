2. Nature of the service

The Readings are an interpretive and reflective practice. They combine an intuitive reading of the client's field, a symbolic analysis (using cards as a symbolic language) and the client's charts (birth chart and Human Design chart), to explore the moment and the theme the client shares, with perspectives, questions and practical suggestions.

The Readings and sessions:

  • are not a prediction of the future and do not determine events;
  • are not a diagnosis or medical, psychological, legal or financial advice;
  • do not replace psychological, psychiatric or medical care;
  • do not guarantee specific personal, professional or financial results.

What is presented in the Reading and in the session is intended to support the client's own reflection. Decisions, and how they are put into practice, always remain the client's responsibility.

3. Formats, service description and process

On the form, the client shares the theme and context, in writing or by dictating. For the Essence Reading, the client also provides the date, time and place of birth; if the time of birth is unknown, the client should contact Filipa Osório before purchasing. For the Now Reading, birth details are optional: if provided, the client's charts are included in the Reading. The stand-alone session does not require birth details.

3.1 Now Reading

A personalised reading focused on a specific question or situation, exploring what is holding back what the client wishes for and what the next step could be. It combines the field reading, the symbolic analysis and, if birth details are provided, a brief look at the client's charts. It is delivered in audio, with a written action proposal (on a Notion page), within 7 business days after payment is confirmed and the theme and context are received. It works as an asynchronous session and does not include a live session. The service is complete once the audio and the written action proposal have been delivered.

3.2 Now Reading + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.1 and, after the Reading is delivered, a one-hour individual online session with Filipa Osório to explore what the Reading brought up. The session is booked after the Reading is delivered. This option is complete once the session has taken place.

3.3 Essence Reading

  • a personalised reading of the client's charts, guided by the theme and context shared, about their unique way of acting, deciding, receiving and caring for their energy, and about repeating patterns and beliefs;
  • questions, observation prompts and small practices;
  • Reiki sent to the theme shared.

The Reading is delivered in writing within 14 business days after payment is confirmed and all necessary details are received. It does not include a live meeting. The service is complete once the written Reading and any contracted complements have been delivered.

3.4 Essence Reading + your current yearly cycle

Includes everything described in 3.3 and an additional written Reading about the client's yearly cycle, related to their charts and the moment they are living. If the client's birthday is close (about one month away or less), the new cycle is analysed; otherwise, the current cycle is analysed. Both Readings are delivered within the period set out in 3.3. This option is complete once both Readings have been delivered. The yearly cycle can also be added to the Essence Reading with an alignment session (€369).

3.5 Essence Reading + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.3 and, after the Reading is delivered, a one-hour individual online session with Filipa Osório to explore the themes recognised and work on the relevant beliefs. The session is booked after the Reading is delivered. This option is complete once the session has taken place.

3.6 Stand-alone alignment session

A one-hour individual online session with Filipa Osório, without a prior Reading, starting directly from the theme the client wants to work on. The session lasts exactly one hour. After payment is confirmed, the client is taken to the booking page (Cal.com) to choose the date and time, and also receives the link by email. This option is complete once the session has taken place.

3.7 Sessions offered after a Reading

After a Reading is delivered, Filipa Osório may offer the client an alignment session on specific terms, through a personal payment link. Those terms (price and deadline) are stated in the offer itself, and the session follows 3.6 and section 5.

3.8 Readings not carried out at the provider's initiative

I reserve the right not to start, or to stop, preparing a Reading before it is delivered if I consider that the conditions to provide the service with due care and suitability are not met. In that case, I will inform the client by email and refund in full the amount paid for the services that will not be provided, at no cost to the client.

3.9 Reiki included in the Readings

The Readings include Reiki sent to the theme shared by the client. In this service, Reiki is presented as sending energy of unconditional love: a moment of intention, presence and care that accompanies the Reading and its integration. It is a complementary spiritual practice, within the interpretive and reflective nature of these services described in section 2.

5. Rescheduling and cancelling the alignment session

  • The client chooses the date and time through the booking page (Cal.com), to which the client is taken right after payment has been confirmed (or, for the formats that include a Reading, after the Reading has been delivered). Rescheduling and cancelling are done through the booking link itself or by message to Filipa Osório.
  • The client may reschedule the session up to 48 hours before the scheduled time at no cost.
  • With less than 48 hours’ notice, or in the event of a missed session without notice, rescheduling costs €30. The new date is only booked once that payment has been confirmed.
  • If the rescheduling fee is not paid within 30 days of the request or of the missed session, the session is deemed to have taken place, with no refund.
  • Cancelling the stand-alone session: up to 48 hours before the scheduled time, the client may cancel and receive a full refund of the amount paid. With less than 48 hours’ notice, or in the event of a missed session without notice, no refund is due, but the client may reschedule as set out above.
  • Filipa Osório applies the same standard of flexibility to herself: if she needs to reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances or force majeure, she will give as much notice as possible, at no cost to the client.

6. Right of withdrawal and refunds

Preparation begins within 24 hours after payment, receipt of all required details and the client’s express request to begin during the withdrawal period. Starting preparation does not, by itself, remove statutory withdrawal rights.

For a service contract concluded at a distance, a client acting as a consumer generally has 14 days from the conclusion of the contract to withdraw, under Portuguese Decree-Law No. 24/2014. The client may withdraw by sending an unequivocal statement to mail@filipaosorio.com.

  • Before work starts: if the client withdraws within the statutory period and preparation has not started, the amount paid is refunded.
  • Providing details and starting preparation: personalised preparation begins after payment, receipt of the necessary details and context, and the client’s express request to start within the withdrawal period. Consent is collected separately on the form, without a preselected option. Providing details alone does not remove consumer rights.
  • Withdrawal after work has started: if the client expressly requested an early start and withdraws before completion, only an amount proportionate to the work actually performed up to the withdrawal notice is due. The remainder is refunded.
  • After full performance: the statutory right of withdrawal no longer applies once the service has been fully performed following the client’s express consent and prior acknowledgement that full performance will end that right. For Essence, completion means delivery of the written Reading and contracted complementary materials; for the Now Reading, delivery of the audio and the written action proposal. A purchased session that has not taken place is not automatically treated as performed when the Reading is delivered.

Where due, a refund is made within the statutory 14 days after notice, using the same payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise without additional cost to the client. After the statutory period, cancellations follow the conditions of the purchased service, without prejudice to statutory rights where a service is not performed or is defective.

Gifted Readings: the purchaser is the client. The same withdrawal conditions apply. Consent to use the recipient’s details must be obtained before sharing them. The gift card is valid for one month from purchase; this does not remove mandatory statutory rights.

These conditions respect Articles 10, 12, 15 and 17 of Portuguese Decree-Law No. 24/2014.
