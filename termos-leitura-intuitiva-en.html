2. Nature of the service

Essence Reading is a spiritual and interpretive practice of self-knowledge. It uses the birth chart and Human Design as symbolic languages to explore the moment and the theme the client shares, offering perspectives, questions and practical suggestions.

Essence Reading:

  • is not a prediction of the future and does not determine events;
  • is not a diagnosis or medical, psychological, legal or financial advice;
  • does not replace psychological, psychiatric or medical support;
  • does not guarantee specific personal, professional or financial outcomes.

What is presented in the Reading and in the session is intended to support the client’s own reflection. Decisions, and how they are put into practice, always remain the client’s responsibility.

3. Formats, service description and process

For Essence and Solar Return readings, the client provides their date, time and place of birth, together with their theme and context. If the client does not know their time of birth, they should speak with Filipa Osório before purchasing. Now Readings and stand-alone sessions do not require birth details.

3.1 Essence Reading

  • a personalized Reading of the client’s birth chart and Human Design, guided by the theme and context they share;
  • a short personalized symbolic story connected to the client’s moment, which gives shape to the themes explored;

The Reading is delivered within 14 business days of confirmation of payment and receipt of all the necessary information. The Reading and the story are delivered in writing, the audio is an integration complement, and the base Reading does not include a live meeting. The service is considered fully performed once the written Reading and the contracted complementary materials have been delivered.

3.2 Essence Reading + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.1 and, after the Reading has been delivered, a one-hour, one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório to go deeper into the themes the client recognized, work on the relevant beliefs and, if the client wishes, explore their Human Design chart in more depth. The session is scheduled after the Reading has been delivered. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.3 Essence Reading + Solar Return

Includes everything described in 3.1 and an additional written Reading dedicated to the client’s yearly cycle (Solar Return), related to their birth chart and the moment they are living. If the client’s birthday is near (about 1 month before or less), the new cycle is analyzed; otherwise, the current cycle is analyzed. Both Readings are delivered within the timeframe set out in 3.1. This format is considered complete once both Readings have been delivered.

3.4 Essence Reading + Solar Return + alignment session

Includes everything described in 3.1 and 3.3 and, after the Readings have been delivered, a one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório, as set out in 3.2. The session is not scheduled at the time of purchase: date options are sent after the Reading has been delivered. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.5 Stand-alone alignment (and deepening) session

A one-to-one online session with Filipa Osório, lasting 1 hour, without a prior Reading, which starts directly from the theme the client wants to work on. The session is not scheduled at the time of purchase: once payment has been confirmed, the client is taken to the booking page (Cal.com) to choose the date and time, and also receives the link by email. This format is considered complete once the session has taken place.

3.6 Reading not carried out at the provider’s initiative

I reserve the right not to begin, or to interrupt, the preparation of the Reading before it is delivered, if I consider that the conditions are not in place to provide the service with the necessary care and suitability. In that case, I will inform the client by e-mail and fully refund the amount paid for the services that will not be provided, at no cost to the client.

3.7 The Now Reading

A personalised Tarot reading focused on a specific question or situation. The client provides their name, contact details, preferred language, theme and context. Birth details are not required. Delivery is by audio within five business days after payment and receipt of the complete context. The base reading does not include a live session; a one-hour alignment session may be added at checkout for €109. The base service is complete when the audio is delivered; an additional session remains outstanding until it takes place.

Essence and the Now Reading are separate services. The Now Reading uses cards as a symbolic language for reflection and is subject to the same confidentiality, personal-use and responsibility provisions in these Terms.

3.8 Reiki included in the Readings

Essence and the Now Reading include Reiki dedicated to the theme shared by the client. Here, Reiki is a transmission of the energy of unconditional love: a moment of intention, presence and care that accompanies the Reading and its integration. It is a complementary spiritual practice within the reflective and interpretive nature of the Services described in section 2.

  • Before work starts: if the client withdraws within the statutory period and preparation has not started, the amount paid is refunded.
  • Providing details and starting preparation: personalised preparation begins after payment, receipt of the necessary details and context, and the client’s express request to start within the withdrawal period. Consent is collected separately on the form, without a preselected option. Providing details alone does not remove consumer rights.
  • Withdrawal after work has started: if the client expressly requested an early start and withdraws before completion, only an amount proportionate to the work actually performed up to the withdrawal notice is due. The remainder is refunded.

A purchased session that has not taken place is not automatically treated as performed when the Reading is delivered.

Where due, a refund is made within the statutory 14 days after notice, using the same payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise without additional cost to the client. After the statutory period, cancellations follow the conditions of the purchased service, without prejudice to statutory rights where a service is not performed or is defective.
